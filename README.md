# Pranav Choudhari — UI/UX Designer & Creative Technologist Portfolio

A modern, high-performance, award-style portfolio website designed specifically for **Pranav Choudhari**, Senior UI/UX Designer and Creative Technologist based in Pune, India.

## Design Philosophy & Visual System
- **Editorial Typography**: Pairing of modern sans-serif (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) with expressive editorial italics (*Instrument Serif*) and high-legibility tabular numerals (*JetBrains Mono*).
- **Refined Color Palette**: Warm off-white/ivory foundation (`#F7F7F2`), deep charcoal primary type (`#121314`), and a signature electric lime accent (`#B7FF2A`).
- **60-30-10 Rule**: 60% neutral canvas, 30% structural surfaces & media cards, 10% high-intent focal accents.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Custom magnetic cursor on desktop, smooth scroll-triggered count-up metrics, horizontal project drag/swipe carousel, and fluid FAQ accordion.

## Structure & Sections
1. **Header**: 3-zone contract with brand wordmark, clean typography links, dark/light theme toggle, and CTA.
2. **Hero**: 90vh editorial layout with headline typography reveal, active status indicator, positioning statements, and portrait composition with floating role badges.
3. **Metrics**: Real tabular count-up for 2.5+ Years Experience, 15+ Projects, 500+ UI Screens, 10K+ Art Community.
4. **Philosophy**: Statement highlighting transformation of messy problems into useful, scalable digital products with core competency chips.
5. **Selected Work**: Interactive horizontal carousel featuring **CuroCare**, **T-Mobile**, **Altleads**, and **DDE** with deep-dive modal case studies.
6. **Process**: Connected 4-stage track line illustrating Discover, Define, Design, Deliver.
7. **Services**: Expandable interactive service rows covering Product UX, Interface Design, and Design Systems.
8. **About & Story**: Mechanical Engineering to UI/UX journey narrative, sticky portrait, interactive career timeline, and "DESIGN × ART" identity.
9. **Tools & Craft**: Grid of tools (Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator, Framer, AI Tools, Developer Handoff).
10. **Domain Expertise**: Healthcare UX, Agentic AI, Enterprise SaaS, Art & Creativity.
11. **FAQ**: Accessible accordion with rotation indicator answering key client and recruiter queries.
12. **Contact**: Dramatic dark CTA section with direct email trigger and inquiry form.
13. **Footer**: Minimalist site directory, copyright, and circular scroll-progress back-to-top ring.

## Architecture
- **Vanilla Web Stack**: Semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 with custom properties, and modular Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Zero Heavy Framework Overhead**: Super-fast initial load with zero client-side hydration delays.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AA contrast, semantic landmarks, skip links, aria-expanded states, keyboard navigability, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.
