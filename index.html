02. PROCESS

How I Turn Chaos Into Clarity

A disciplined four-phase product design framework bridging user empathy, architectural problem-definition, and pixel-precise execution.

01

Discover

Understand users, goals and constraints. Conducting stakeholder interviews, contextual inquiries, and competitive audits to uncover core behavioral bottlenecks.

02

Define

Structure problems and identify opportunities. Synthesizing research insights into clear journey maps, persona matrices, and technical feasibility parameters.

03

Design

Create flows, wireframes and interfaces. Rapidly exploring low-fidelity interaction patterns before sculpting high-fidelity, accessible UI component ecosystems.

04

Deliver

Prototype, document and collaborate with developers. Providing atomic design tokens, motion choreographies, and edge-case state specs for zero-drift implementation.
